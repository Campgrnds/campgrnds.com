03Selling into Europe

ESPR, on one page

If you sell clothing, accessories, or footwear in the EU, the ESPR will shape what you disclose, how you handle unsold stock, and eventually what travels with every product as a digital passport. Here's where things stand.

01Jul 2024

In force

The Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 sets the framework for product rules, including digital product passports.

0219 Jul 2026

No destroying unsold stock

Large companies can no longer destroy unsold apparel, clothing accessories, or footwear. Medium-sized companies follow in July 2030. Micro and small companies are exempt.

03Mar 2027

Standard reporting

Large companies report discarded unsold products in a standardized format.

042027 · indicative

Textile rules

A delegated act for textiles and apparel is expected, setting passport and ecodesign requirements. Rules typically apply about 18 months after adoption.

Current as of October 2026. Dates marked indicative depend on the European Commission's working plan and can move. Sources: Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 (ESPR) and the ESPR 2025–2030 working plan.
