01What it was

The garment, remembered.

The Campgrnds passport was a living record bound to a physical piece: materials, origin, care, authenticity, and the story of the people who made it. For the customer it was proof and provenance in one scan. For the brand it kept working after the sale, through resale, repair, and re-engagement.

02How it worked

Four steps

01

Mint

Each piece or production run got a verifiable record.

02

Bind

The record was attached to the garment through its tag, so the item and its history traveled together.

03

Scan

A scan revealed provenance, styling, care, and unlockable experiences. No app needed.

04

Grow

Resales, repairs, and scans fed back to the brand and extended the relationship past the first sale.

04What it proved

What it did
for the house

Ready for regulation

Passports are where EU rules for textiles are heading. Building one early showed what the data actually takes.

Resale with the brand in it

Authenticated resale blocks counterfeits and can route royalties back to the brand.

Post-purchase storytelling

Scans delivered campaign films, styling notes, and drop announcements.

Counterfeit protection

One-to-one binding made every authentic piece verifiable.
