Voice
Agents

A concierge for the brand that never slept. It answered, booked, and sold in the brand's voice.

Channels Phone · Web · WhatsAppHours 24/7Handoff Human
01What it was

The brand, on the line.

Each agent was a trained extension of the studio. It worked the phone line, the website, and WhatsApp, and it spoke the brand's language about materials, fit, and craft. It qualified callers, answered sizing and stock questions from the live catalog, booked fittings, and handed the conversation to a person when it should.
