I'm a fashion technologist and AI solutions architect. I spent 2021 to 2024 building Campgrnds. Now I help designers plan that kind of work for themselves.

01The story

Fashion technology is about communication. Campgrnds was my way of letting a garment keep talking to the person who wears it, through NFC tags, digital product passports, and AI voice agents.

I started Campgrnds in 2021 under Brown-Webster Blockchain Technologies Group Inc. Over the next three years we debuted a digital product passport MVP at Samsung 837, sent NFC-enabled pieces down a Harlem runway during New York Fashion Week, and built AI voice agents for small fashion houses.

That company is winding down. The Campgrnds name, the work, and everything I learned building it are mine, and I put them to use as an independent consultant. I don't build systems for brands anymore. I show designers how to do it, what it takes, and how to stay compliant while they sell into Europe.
